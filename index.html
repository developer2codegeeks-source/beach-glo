Tan Prep & Aftercare Guide

Quick tips to help your color develop evenly and last longer after every session at Beach Glo.

1. Exfoliate Gently

Exfoliate the night before your appointment so your tan develops smoothly and evenly.

2. Arrive With Clean Skin

Skip heavy oils, perfumes, or makeup prior to stepping into the bed or Red Light booth.

3. Use Indoor Tanning Lotion

Hydrated skin tans faster and deeper. Ask our team in-salon for lotion recommendations.

4. Bring Eye Protection

Always wear protective eyewear during UV sessions—we have options available at the front desk.

1. Moisturize Daily

Lock in your glow with a rich, tan-extending moisturizer morning and night.

2. Stay Hydrated

Drinking plenty of water keeps skin supple and extends the life of your bronze color.

3. Wait Before Showering

Give bronzing lotions time to settle—wait a few hours before showering for best results.

4. Pair With Red Light

Alternate UV sessions with Red Light Therapy to keep skin looking smooth and refreshed.
